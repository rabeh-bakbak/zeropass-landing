The vision

A connected world needs a new foundation.

Identity systems exist across countries, institutions and industries. But trust does not always travel with them. ZeroPass aims to make independent systems work together without forcing them into one.

⌘

Interoperability

Connect existing identity ecosystems through common, open coordination standards rather than replacing them.

◇

Neutral coordination

Enable independent participants to coordinate trust without depending on a single global controlling authority.

⬡

Privacy by design

Make data minimization a foundational requirement. Trust coordination should not require a universal identity database.

Our principles

Build the foundation.
Not another silo.

ZeroPass is being developed around a simple conviction: global trust should not depend on one platform, one identity provider or one institution. Its long-term direction is an independently operated network guided by open standards, accountable governance and verifiable security.

What ZeroPass stands for

Trust should be verifiable.

The objective is not to centralize identity. It is to establish a common coordination layer through which independent systems can evaluate trust according to transparent rules.

◎

No single point of control

A governance and operational model designed to distribute responsibility and reduce systemic dependency.

⌁

Designed to evolve

Open architecture and replaceable technical components to support responsible long-term evolution.

✓

Security as a foundation

Security, privacy, accountability and independent review are essential design requirements, not marketing promises.
